Finance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Finance Tracker is a personal budget tracker. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

There is no server

Finance Tracker is a static web app with no backend of any kind. Nobody — not the developer, not any company — receives, stores, or has access to your financial data. There is nowhere for it to be sent, because the app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Where your data actually lives

  • By default: in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), on the device you're using. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Drive backup.
  • If you turn on "Back up to Drive": in a single hidden file, inside a private folder in your own Google Drive account — one that only this app can see, using Google's restricted drive.appdata scope. This app cannot see, list, or modify any other file in your Drive, and no one other than you can access that hidden folder either.

What Google sign-in is used for

Signing in with Google is only used to (1) confirm which Google account should read and write that one private backup file, and (2) show your email address in the app so you know which account is connected. That's the entire purpose of the permission request — nothing broader is requested, and nothing is used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than syncing your own data to your own Drive.

No tracking, no ads, no selling data

This app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and does not sell or share data with any third party, because there is no data on any server to sell or share in the first place.

Deleting your data

Clear your browser's site data to remove the local copy. To remove the Drive backup, open the app, click the sync status in the header, and choose Disconnect — or revoke access directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections, which also deletes the app's private folder.
