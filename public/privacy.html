Finance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This app is currently operated by an individual developer, not a registered company. Sections marked [to be confirmed] below depend on decisions only the developer can make (legal entity, governing jurisdiction) and have not been finalized.

Finance Tracker is a personal budget tracker. This page explains, as precisely as the code allows, what happens to your data.

There is no server

Finance Tracker is a static web app with no backend of any kind. Nobody — not the developer, not any company — receives, stores, or has access to your financial data on a server, because there is no server for it to be sent to. The application code that runs in your browser is served as static files; it does not transmit your financial entries anywhere on its own.

What personal data this app handles

No name, address, phone number, payment details, or government ID is collected anywhere in this app. There is no account-creation form of its own — the only sign-in is the optional Google sign-in described below.

Where your data actually lives

  • By default: in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), on the device you're using. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Drive backup.
  • If you turn on "Back up to Drive": in a single hidden file, inside a private folder in your own Google Drive account — one that only this app can see, using Google's restricted drive.appdata scope. This app cannot see, list, or modify any other file in your Drive, and no one other than you can access that hidden folder either.

What Google sign-in is used for

Signing in with Google requests the openid email profile and drive.appdata scopes, used only to (1) confirm which Google account should read and write that one private backup file, and (2) show your email address in the app so you know which account is connected. Nothing broader is requested, and nothing is used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than syncing your own data to your own Drive.

The Google sign-in script itself is only loaded into your browser the first time you click "Back up to Drive" (or, for a returning user who already connected, once per visit to check whether your sign-in is still valid) — not on every page load, and never for a visitor who doesn't use that feature.

Local storage and cookies

This app does not set or read any HTTP cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for a short list of functional values (theme preference, which currency you're viewing, a dismissed-banner flag, the Google token described above, and internal sync bookkeeping) — see the Cookies & Local Storage page for the full, itemized list and why no cookie-consent banner is shown.

Third-party requests

Loading the app makes network requests to a small, fixed set of domains, all disclosed here:

  • fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com — loads the "Instrument Sans" typeface, on every visit. This exposes your IP address and browser information to Google as a normal side effect of fetching a font file from Google's servers; the app does not otherwise instruct Google to track you through this request.
  • accounts.google.com and Google's Drive/OAuth APIs — only if and when you use "Back up to Drive," as described above.

This app uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind, and does not sell or share data with any third party for their own purposes.

Deleting your data

Clear your browser's site data to remove the local copy. To remove the Drive backup, open the app, click the sync status in the header, and choose Disconnect — or revoke access directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections, which also deletes the app's private folder.
