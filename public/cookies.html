Finance Tracker — Cookies & Local Storage

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This app does not use cookies

Verified directly against the source code: nothing in this app reads or writes an HTTP cookie (document.cookie is never used). Everything below is your browser's local storage and IndexedDB instead — different, older-web technologies that work similarly (they persist small amounts of data on your device between visits) but are not literally cookies.

Why there's no cookie-consent banner

Every value this app stores locally is what regulators typically call "strictly necessary" — each one exists only to make a feature you directly asked for work (remembering your theme, keeping you signed in to your own Drive backup, remembering which currency you were viewing). None of it is used for advertising, cross-site tracking, analytics, or profiling, and nothing is shared with a third party for their own purposes. Under the common EU/UK ePrivacy "strictly necessary" exemption, storage like this generally does not require a consent banner. This is this app's own assessment based on that widely-used exemption, not a certified legal opinion — if you plan to serve users in a jurisdiction with stricter rules, have a qualified lawyer confirm this conclusion before relying on it.

Third-party cookies you might encounter

This app does not set third-party cookies. If you use "Back up to Drive," Google's own sign-in page (which this app does not control) may set cookies belonging to Google, governed by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

How to clear everything

Clearing your browser's site data for this app removes all local storage and the IndexedDB database in one step. Disconnecting Drive backup from the sync menu (or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/connections) removes the stored Google token and stops the app from syncing.
